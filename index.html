DALI Occupancy Detector Configuration

The detector must be in configuration mode. The BLE connection is encrypted, and settings are saved to non-volatile storage.

Not connected.

DALI profile
Night dimming steps

Step times are virtual: 00:00 is the middle of the night, estimated from the power-on durations of the last 5 nights (12 h assumed before the first measured night). A step applies from its time until the next enabled step or power-off; before the first step, the DALI profile levels above apply. Enabled steps must be in chronological order.

BLE radio

After writing, the page reads the saved bytes back and compares them with the requested values. Operating and BLE settings are applied when the detector returns to normal mode. DALI transceiver GPIOs are fixed in the firmware and cannot be changed here.
